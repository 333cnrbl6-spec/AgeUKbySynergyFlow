# Public Data Sources for Age UK Bury Prospect Lists

## Overview
This document catalogues freely-available, public-domain datasets that can be used to populate and validate prospect lists for Age UK Bury's services. All sources listed are official government data with open licensing (typically OGL 3.0).

---

## 1. **ONS Census 2021 – Bury-Specific Demographics**
**Source:** Office for National Statistics (ONS)  
**URL:** https://www.ons.gov.uk/visualisations/censusareachanges/E08000002/  
**License:** OGL 3.0 (Open Government License)  
**Last Updated:** 19 January 2023  
**Data Quality:** OFFICIAL – High reliability

### Available Data:
- **Population by Age Group (Bury, 2021):**
  - 50–64 years: 19.7% of population (~38,200 people)
  - 65–74 years: 10.0% of population (~19,400 people)
  - 75–84 years: 6.1% of population (~11,800 people)
  - 85+ years: 2.2% of population (~4,300 people)
  - **Total 50+: ~73,700 residents** (38% of Bury's 193,900 population)

- **Trend (2011–2021):**
  - 50–64 age group grew by 3,600 (+10.3%)
  - 65–74 age group grew by 1,200 (+13.6%)
  - Overall population growth: +4.8% (Bury), +5.2% (North West)

- **Housing Tenure (relevant for handyperson outreach):**
  - Owner-occupiers: 66.9% (may benefit from home maintenance)
  - Private rented: 17.7%
  - Social rented: 14.9%

- **Unpaid Care (proxy for health/mobility needs):**
  - 4.5% provide 1–19 hrs/week unpaid care
  - 2.0% provide 20–49 hrs/week
  - 2.9% provide 50+ hrs/week
  - Total caring population: ~9.4%

### Use Case:
- **Validation:** Cross-check prospect demographic claims
- **Segmentation:** Target owner-occupiers for handyperson services
- **Outreach:** Identify wards with highest 75+ concentration
- **Reporting:** Baseline for grant applications & impact reports

### Downloadable Data:
- Full age-group table as CSV (available from ONS page)
- PNG charts for reporting/presentations

## 3. **ONS Age-Specific Datasets (Continuous)**
**Source:** Office for National Statistics – Ageing Research Portal  
**URL:** https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/birthsdeathsandmarriages/ageing/datalist?filter=datasets  
**License:** OGL 3.0  
**Data Quality:** OFFICIAL

### Available Datasets:
1. **Mid-year population estimates of the very old (90+)** – England, Wales, UK
   - Annual updates
   - Single-year age breakdowns
   - Sex-disaggregated

2. **Older people in care homes, Census 2021** – England & Wales
   - Care home resident counts by age group
   - Living arrangement patterns
   - Cross-tabulation with health conditions

3. **Centenarians relationship to household members**
   - Family structure of 100+ age group
   - Identifies potential carers/support networks

### Use Case:
- **Dementia Support Outreach:** Use care home data to identify potential service users
- **Isolated Older Adults:** Identify lone-person households in public data
- **Impact Reports:** Cite national trends to contextualize Bury performance

## 5. **UK Data Service – Census Archive (1961–2021)**
**Source:** UK Data Service (UKDS)  
**URL:** https://ukdataservice.ac.uk/learning-hub/census/  
**License:** OGL 3.0 / Various  
**Data Quality:** OFFICIAL – Academic-curated

### Available Data:
- Census data from all UK censuses (1961–2021)
- Aggregate data at various geographies (local authority, ward, MSOA)
- Microdata (anonymized individual records) – requires registration
- Boundary files and mapping resources

### Use Case:
- **Deep Dives:** Access ward-level age breakdowns within Bury (higher granularity than ONS top-level)
- **Historical Trends:** Analyze 50-year aging trends in specific Bury wards
- **Research:** Academic-quality census analysis for funding bids
- **Mapping:** Use boundary files to create service coverage maps

## Data Integration Strategy for Prospect Lists

### Phase 1: Validation (Immediate)
1. Use **Census 2021 Bury data** to:
   - Validate prospect demographic self-reports
   - Cross-check postcode coverage against age distribution
   - Identify under-represented wards

2. Use **Council Adult Social Care reports** to:
   - Understand referral volumes & waiting times
   - Align prospect targeting to ASC need types

### Phase 2: Segmentation (Medium-term)
1. Cross-tabulate Census 2021 **age groups + housing tenure**:
   - Owner-occupiers 65+ = handyperson service prospects
   - Social rented 75+ = befriending/support prospects

2. Use **Bury wards with highest 75+ density** for targeted outreach

### Phase 3: Impact Reporting (Ongoing)
1. Reference **ONS Census data** as baseline in grant reports:
   - "Age UK Bury serves [X]% of Bury's 73,700 residents aged 50+"
   - "Services address care gaps identified in Bury Council ASC strategy"

2. Cite **national aging datasets** for context:
   - "In line with national trends, Bury's 65–74 age group grew 13.6% since 2011"
