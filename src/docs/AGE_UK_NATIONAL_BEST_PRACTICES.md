## 3. **Befriending & Isolation Support** (Major National Focus)

### Age UK National Model
- **Befriending services:** In-person visits + phone/video calls
- **The Silver Line:** 24/7 free helpline (managed nationally; local partners deliver)
- **Group activities** (cafes, day centres, exercise classes)
- **Technology:** Help older people get online & stay connected

### Age UK Bury Adaptation
1. **Expand befriending capacity:**
   - Recruit volunteer befrienders (current volunteers + expansion)
   - Offer phone befriending for housebound clients
   - Introduce video befriending (Zoom/Teams) for tech-comfortable clients
   - Track hours & outcomes in system

2. **Partner with Silver Line:**
   - Publicize 0800 716 1616 (24/7 confidential helpline)
   - Train staff to recommend for evening/weekend support

3. **Social activities calendar:**
   - Replicate Age UK's event calendar on Bury website
   - Include: weekly cafes, exercise classes, day trips, hobby groups
   - Publish in-app & on local website

4. **IT training:**
   - Offer basic digital skills (email, video calls, WhatsApp)
   - Reduce isolation through technology

**Tracking:** Add fields to Client entity for befriending hours, visit frequency, loneliness score (baseline/progress).

## 4. **Handyperson Service Excellence** (Core Strength)

### Age UK National Best Practice
- Safety-focused (not just repairs—assess fall risks, security, accessibility)
- Quality assurance (customer feedback, repeat jobs, referrals)
- Integration with other services (nutritionist, occupational therapy, social prescribing)
- Cost transparency (free to eligible, sliding scale for others)

### Age UK Bury Enhancement
1. **Service standardization:**
   - Publish service standards & pricing
   - Create job scope checklist (scope creep prevention)
   - Customer feedback form (in-app or paper)

2. **Safety assessments:**
   - Handyperson as "eyes on ground"
   - Flag hazards (poor lighting, trip risks, heating issues)
   - Link to Age UK Bury safety audit

3. **Quality metrics:**
   - Job completion rate
   - Customer satisfaction score
   - Referral rate (customer recommends to friend)
   - Return visits (repeat business)

4. **Marketing:**
   - Case studies (before/after, testimonials)
   - Partner with GP surgeries, NHS, council social care teams

## 6. **Service Integration & Referral Pathways**

### Age UK National Model
Services are **integrated**, not siloed:
- Handyperson visit → flags isolation → befriending referral
- Befriending → identifies housing/health need → advice line + council referral
- Day centre → social + exercise + nutrition + health checks

### Age UK Bury Implementation
1. **Holistic client profile:**
   - Link Client → Services received (jobs, activities, befriending)
   - Capture presenting needs on intake
   - Flag unmet needs (e.g., isolation, housing, benefits)

2. **Service referral workflow:**
   - After handyperson job: "Would client benefit from befriending?"
   - After befriending: "Any health/housing concerns?"
   - After day centre: "Any follow-up needed?"

3. **Partner referral network:**
   - Create formal referral pathway with Bury Council ASC
   - Build relationship with NHS (GP surgeries, ICB)
   - Partner with housing + benefits advice services

## 8. **Marketing & Communication Strategy**

### Age UK National Approach
1. **Tone:** Hopeful, empowering, not patronizing
2. **Channels:** Website, email, social (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram), partnerships
3. **Content:** Mix of information, stories, campaigns, events
4. **Call-to-action:** Donate, volunteer, use services, get advice

### Age UK Bury Replication
1. **Social media calendar:**
   - 2–3 posts/week (mix of tips, events, impact stories)
   - Highlight: benefits advice, safety tips, activity calendar, volunteer spotlights

2. **Email newsletter:**
   - Monthly (or bi-weekly if high engagement)
   - Sections: Featured service, tip of the month, event calendar, volunteer spotlight, donate CTA

3. **Local partnerships:**
   - GP surgeries (posters, referral cards)
   - Bury Council (social care, housing)
   - NHS (ICB, community teams)
   - Libraries, community centres

4. **Annual campaign:**
   - Align with national themes (e.g., Age UK campaign calendars)
   - Example: "Winter wellness" (Nov–Feb), "Summer activities" (Jun–Aug)

## Conclusion
Age UK National's strength lies in **holistic, integrated service delivery**—not just repairs or befriending, but a **ecosystem** where clients access multiple services tailored to their needs. Age UK Bury can replicate this by:

1. **Expanding service portfolio** (foot care, home help, IT training)
2. **Integrating referral pathways** (handyperson → befriending; day centre → health check)
3. **Communicating impact** (newsletters, stories, events)
4. **Building partnerships** (council, NHS, volunteers)
5. **Leveraging technology** (in-app bookings, events, guides)

**Success metric:** Increase average services per client from current ~1.5 to 3+ (e.g., client gets job done + befriending + day centre activity).
